This Pluriverse is a dreamscape, container and movement for collective wisdom

This Pluriverse holds a shapeshifting world, finding space to confront inherited wounds and opportunities to dream otherwise. Old certainties about who we are, what is holy and what we owe one another are trembling and we can feel it. We don’t want to look away, so we acknowledge the weight of it, name it honestly and together, find ways to refuse despair.

Approach

Vision

To create the conditions for new questions and answers to emerge and nurture the embodiment of multiple truths, one that honours our lived, diverse and marginalised ways of knowing ourselves and being the ones who will build new worlds.  

What is the spirit of the work?

We resurface so that feeling becomes medicine, embodiment becomes resistance, collaboration and creativity become infrastructures of epistemic flourishing that resist capture. Our movement is grounded in traditions of knowledge as liberatory, gathering as world-making, and dreaming as the labour of our collective freedom and flourishing.

How do we exist in the world?

We move in this world in many different ways: through gathering, workshops, teach-ins, storytelling, music, art and play, memory and knowledge-sharing. No matter how we show up, we remain guided by this yearning.
